# UncivWarcraft

A standalone base ruleset that turns Unciv into the world of Warcraft: the races of Azeroth as playable factions, a tech tree that runs through Azeroth's history, and Warcraft wonders, units and resources.

**Status: alpha.** Every race has its own leader, ability, cities and AI, plus unique units and a unique building (the Humans have the most). Every race has its own look on the map, though: each foot, mounted and crewed unit is redrawn per race (bearded, stocky Dwarves; small Gnomes; tall, long-eared elves; tusked Trolls in varied skin tones; green, muscular Orcs; horned Tauren and Draenei; Pandaren; Worgen; Forsaken; Goblins). Mounted units ride each race's own beast: wolves, rams, mechanostriders, nightsabers, kodos, raptors, hawkstriders, elekks, dragon turtles, goblin trikes, skeletal horses, white steeds and Gilnean horses.

## What's in it

**Eras** follow Azeroth's timeline: Age of Tribes → Age of Arathor → Age of the Dark Portal → Age of the Third War → Age of the Lich King → Age of the Cataclysm → Age of the Legion → Age of Azerite → Age of the Void.

**15 races**, each with a leader, unique ability, AI personality and city list: Humans, Orcs, Night Elves, Dwarves, Gnomes, Tauren, Trolls, Blood Elves, High Elves, Pandaren, Forsaken, Draenei, Goblins, Worgen, and **the Scourge** under Arthas, the Lich King, ruling from Icecrown Citadel in Northrend.

**Humans (complete)** — led by Anduin Wrynn, in Stormwind blue and gold
- *The Human Spirit:* City-State influence decays 50% slower and rests 20 higher; +15% Great Person generation. *For Lordaeron!* +10% Strength in your own lands. *Call to Arms:* when another civilization declares war on you, 2 free infantry of your current era appear.
- **Footman** (replaces Swordsman): stronger, starts with Defend I, needs no Iron; carries a heater shield with a sunburst.
- **Paladin** (replaces Knight): plate armour and warhammer on a barded white charger. Heals adjacent units, +10% Strength to nearby units (Devotion Aura), +20% when defending (Divine Shield), +25% against the Forsaken.
- **Mage** (replaces Crossbowman): a robed battle-mage with a crystal staff; stronger ranged attack that fires over hills and forests.
- **Priest** (replaces Missionary): white-and-gold robes; adjacent units heal 15 HP more.
- **Steam Tank** (replaces Cannon): no set-up before firing, and can move after attacking.
- **Kul Tiran Man-o'-War** (replaces Frigate): faster and better armed.
- **Archmage** (replaces Great Scientist): named after the archmagi of Dalaran (Antonidas, Khadgar, Jaina…).
- **Chapel of the Light** (replaces Temple): +1 Faith, +1 Culture. **Royal Barracks** (replaces Barracks): new units start with 25 XP, +1 Culture. **Lumber Mill** (replaces Workshop): +1 Production, +1 Production from Forests. **Castle** (replaces Keep): stronger defenses, +1 Culture. **Altar of Kings** (Humans only, one per empire): a free Great General and +15% Great Person generation.
- Every other Human unit wears blue and gold on the map, and Peasants carry lumber.

**Each race plays differently:**
- **Scourge:** the undead eat half as much, Plagued Land gives them +2 Production and +1 Science, fallen enemies can rise to swell their cities, and their key buildings (Necropolis, Crypt, Ziggurat) need blighted ground nearby. Acolytes **spread the blight**. Unique: Ghoul, Acolyte, Death Knight, Frost Wyrm.
- **Forsaken:** Plagued Land feeds them and heals their units; they can spread it too.
- **Night Elves:** forests give +1 Food and are never cut down; **Wisps** replace Peasants.
- **Orcs:** +10% Production and Strength while at war, -10% Culture in peace.
- **Trolls:** regeneration (+10 HP when healing), +1 Faith from jungle, an extra Follower belief.
- **Tauren:** untouched land is sacred: unfarmed plains give Faith, hills without mines give Food and Culture.
- **Dwarves:** **Deep Mines** replace Mines, Titan Ruins give Science and Culture, and some ancient ruins hold Titan relics only dwarves can read.
- **Gnomes:** their own experiment events, with big breakthroughs or explosions.
- **Goblins:** buying costs 20% less Gold and captured cities pay out, but Production is 10% lower.
- **Blood Elves:** mana addiction: -1 Happiness per city from the Age of the Third War until they control Arcane Crystals or the Sunwell is restored.
- **High Elves:** cities are 25% stronger.
- **Draenei:** exiles: a second Settler, a weaker capital, +25% against barbarians and demons, faster missionaries.
- **Worgen:** units shift between human and worgen form (Worgen Stalker ↔ Gilnean Hunter, Gilnean Rifleman ↔ Worgen Ravager), and the curse lets worgen capture units they kill.
- **Pandaren:** in the Age of the Dark Portal they choose the Tushui path (Alliance), the Huojin path (Horde) or neutrality.
- **Alliance and Horde** fight 10% harder against each other.

**Heroes:** every race can field one hero, as in Warcraft III: Uther the Lightbringer, Grom Hellscream, Malfurion Stormrage, Muradin Bronzebeard, Kelsey Steelspark, Baine Bloodhoof, Rokhan, Kael'thas Sunstrider, Alleria Windrunner, Chen Stormstout, Nathanos Blightcaller, Maraad, Gazlowe, Darius Crowley and Kel'Thuzad. Heroes inspire nearby troops, grow stronger with the ages and have their own promotions.

**Travel and magic:** units trained in a city with an **Inn** carry a Hearthstone home; **Mage Towers** attune units to portals between your cities; a **Flight Master** speeds land units through your lands and opens flight paths. **Mana** is gathered by Arcane Sanctums and Mage Towers and spent on mages, arcane missiles and Mana Bombs.

**World threats:** the Scourge marches in the Age of the Lich King, the Legion invades in the Age of the Legion, and the Old Gods whisper in the last ages. The red, blue, bronze, green and black dragonflights may visit with gifts or schemes.

**Unique units and buildings for every race:**
- **Orcs:** Grunt (Swordsman; heals when it kills), Wolf Rider (Outrider; pillages for free, no penalty against cities), Pig Farm (Granary, +1 Production).
- **Trolls:** Headhunter (Ranger; stronger and faster in jungle), Batrider (Gyrocopter; strong against land units), Voodoo Lounge (Shrine, +1 Gold, +1 Culture).
- **Tauren:** Tauren Warrior (Vanguard; much stronger, no Iron), Kodo Beast (Mounted Archer; War Drums aura), Great Totem (Town Hall, +1 Faith).
- **Forsaken:** Abomination (Vanguard; tough, heals on kills, no Iron), Meat Wagon (Catapult; faster), Apothecarium (Herb Garden, +2 Science).
- **Blood Elves:** Spellbreaker (Swordsman; strong against archers and mages), Blood Knight (Knight; heals on kills), Magisters' Sanctum (Arcane Sanctum, +1 Science, +1 Culture).
- **Goblins:** Goblin Shredder (Peasant; faster, sees farther), Goblin Sapper (Cannon; devastating against cities), Goblin Bazaar (Market, +2 Gold).
- **Dwarves:** Ironforge Rifleman (Musketeer; strong in hills), Ram Rider (Knight; keeps terrain defense, strong in hills), Dwarven Forge (Blacksmith, +2 Production).
- **Gnomes:** Mechanostrider Rider (Outrider; 5 movement, no Horses), Gnomish Bombardier (Mortar Team; range 3), Tinker's Workshop (Workshop, +1 Science).
- **Night Elves:** Huntress (Outrider; strong and fast in forests), Glaive Thrower (Ballista; range 3), Moonwell (Shrine, +1 Food).
- **Draenei:** Vindicator (Vanguard; strong against barbarians and demons, heals neighbours), Elekk Rider (Knight; very strong but slow), Crystal Sanctum (Temple, +1 Science).
- **Worgen:** Worgen Stalker (Swordsman; strong and fast in forests, no Iron), Gilnean Rifleman (Rifleman), Gilnean Bulwark (Walls, stronger, +1 Culture).
- **High Elves:** Farstrider (Ranger; strong in forests), Elven Destroyer (War Galley; stronger and faster), Runestone (Walls, stronger, +1 Science).
- **Pandaren:** Brewmaster (Vanguard; strong defender, no Iron), Shado-Pan Monk (Pikeman; 3 movement), Brewery (Granary, +1 Gold, +1 Happiness).
Each has its own map sprite and icon.

**Azeroth world maps:** Kalimdor, the Eastern Kingdoms and Northrend, with every major zone in its place (Elwynn Forest, the Barrens, Dun Morogh, Tanaris, Icecrown and 50+ more), mountain ranges, lakes, rivers, the Maelstrom, Kezan, the Lost Isles and the Wandering Isle. Two sizes: **Azeroth Large** (144 × 88, preselected, room to expand) and **Azeroth** (104 × 64, faster on phones). Each race starts at its capital: Stormwind, Orgrimmar, Darnassus, Ironforge, Gnomeregan, Thunder Bluff, Sen'jin Village, Silvermoon, Quel'Danas, the Temple of Five Dawns on the Wandering Isle, Undercity, the Exodar, Bilgewater Harbor and Gilneas. City-states sit at their real locations.

**Warcraft terrain:** seven special tile types, placed where they belong:
- *Fel-Scorched Land* (Felwood, Blasted Lands): +2 Production, -1 Food; units ending a turn there take 10 damage. Peasants can cleanse it after Priesthood.
- *Plagued Land* (the Plaguelands, Ghostlands, Icecrown, Dragonblight, Zul'Drak): -1 Food, -1 Production until cleansed.
- *Ancient Woods* (Ashenvale, Darkshore, Teldrassil, Moonglade, Hyjal, Feralas, Azshara): night elf forest, +1 Culture, can't be cut down.
- *Bamboo Grove* (the Wandering Isle): 2 Food, 1 Production.
- *Molten Fields* (Burning Steppes, Searing Gorge, Un'Goro): +2 Production, -1 Food, rough going.
- *Crystal Grove* (Crystalsong Forest, Azuremyst and Bloodmyst): +2 Science.
- *Titan Ruins* (Uldaman, Uldum, the Storm Peaks, Sholazar): +1 Science, +1 Culture.

**20 landmarks** (natural wonders) with their own tile art: the Maelstrom, Mount Hyjal, Teldrassil, Moonglade, Lake Elune'ara, Stonetalon Peak, the Red Rocks, Thousand Needles, Un'Goro Crater, the Lakkari Tar Pits, the Lost City of the Tol'vir, Blackrock Mountain, the Cape of Stranglethorn, Kelp'thar Forest, and new: **the Dark Portal**, **the Nexus**, **Galakrond's Rest**, **the Sunken Temple**, **Maraudon** and **the Lifeblood Pillar**.

**City centers in each race's style**, growing from village (Age of Tribes) to town (Age of the Dark Portal) to walled city (Age of the Cataclysm): blue-roofed Stormwind stone and a cathedral for the Humans, spiked red-and-black halls for the Orcs, violet leaf-roofed temples under a great tree for the Night Elves, a forge-lit mountain gate for the Dwarves, gear-and-dome Gnomish workshops, painted tents and a mesa for the Tauren, thatched huts and a stone ziggurat for the Trolls, red-and-gold spires and a golden-ringed tower for the Blood Elves, white-and-blue spires for the High Elves, pagodas for the Pandaren, Lordaeron's ruined keep with fel-green windows for the Forsaken, crystal spires for the Draenei, a goblin bank with smokestacks and a crane, and Gilnean manors with gas lamps for the Worgen. City-states match their founders (Dalaran is violet, Gadgetzan and Booty Bay goblin, Cenarion Hold night elf), and no city ever turns into a modern skyscraper.

**No modern leftovers:** late-era units are Warcraft machines and soldiers rather than tanks and jets: steam golems, dwarven siege engines, gnomish mechano-tanks, mechagnomes, an iron colossus, Horde demolishers, goblin rocket wagons, gnomish flying machines, ironclads and steam warships, an airship tender, an orcish juggernaught, a gnomish submarine and a giant war turtle, plus musket-and-steel infantry (mountaineers, fusiliers, grenadiers, Kul Tiran marines, rocket-pack skyguards). The Titan vessel is built from titan-forged parts. All technology, building, wonder, unit, resource, improvement, policy and air/naval promotion icons are Warcraft-themed. Azerite, Arcane Crystals, Elementium and Thorium have their own map art, the drills are goblin rigs, and farms, mines and forts keep their pre-industrial look in every era. Resources and beliefs use Warcraft names (Kodo, Plainstriders, Star Rubies, Tel'Abim Bananas, Glowcap, Mammoth Ivory, Moonglow Ink, Deeprock Salt; An'she's Light, Neptulon's Tide, Rage of Agamaggan, Whispers of Elune…), and every wonder, technology and unit has a lore entry in the civilopedia. The social policy tree is Warcraft too: Old Kingdoms, Frontier, Blood and Thunder, Devotion, Diplomacy, Trade Princes, Arcane Inquiry, Free Peoples, Dominion and Unity, with policies such as Strom's Legacy, Ready to Work, Lok'tar Ogar, Kor'kron Regulars, Kirin Tor Exchange, Steamwheedle Charter, Patrons of Dalaran and Iron Horde Doctrine.

**Warcraft events:** fifteen random events from Warcraft lore pop up with choices: the **Darkmoon Faire**, the **Plague of Undeath** in your granaries, **Legion Incursions** (a fel rift opens and Felguards or Infernals pour out), **a Goblin Offer** from the Steamwheedle Cartel, the **Lunar Festival**, **Brewfest**, **Children's Week**, **Hallow's End**, **Gnomish Refugees** from Gnomeregan, **a Titan Vault** found by the Explorers' League, **the Cataclysm** (only in the Age of the Cataclysm), **a Wandering Brewmaster**, **an Envoy of the Kirin Tor**, **Murloc Raids** and **the Defias Brotherhood**. Each belongs to its part of the timeline; on average a civilization sees one every 15 turns or so.

**Barbarians are Azeroth's raiders:** murloc packs and gnoll warbands in the early ages, then the red-masked Defias Brotherhood, all with their own sprites; their camps are Defias hideouts dug into old mines. The Burning Legion's **Felguard** and **Infernal** only appear through Legion Incursions.

**Every race's Great People carry that race's names:** an Orc general may be Varok Saurfang or Nazgrim, a Dwarf scientist Brann Bronzebeard, a Goblin merchant Baron Revilgaz, a Night Elf prophet Tyrande Whisperwind. The Great Person improvements are the **Arcane Academy**, **Trade Company**, **Bastion**, **Hall of Heroes** and **Sacred Grounds**; specialists are **Arcanists**, **Traders**, **Bards** and **Tinkers**; city-states are Lorekeeper, Harbor, Mercantile, Militaristic or Sacred, and each speaks with its own voice (Dalaran, Booty Bay, Light's Hope Chapel…). Promotions use Warcraft names too (Deadeye, Heroic Strike, Weapon Mastery, Defend, Spear Wall, Bloodlust, Rapid Fire, Broadsides, Depth Stalker, Skyguard Patrol, Sky Sentinel), ancient ruins and city-state quests are reworded for Azeroth, and every building, resource, improvement, belief, policy and promotion now has a civilopedia entry.

**Faction AI:** every race has its own AI personality. Orcs and Forsaken mass armies, Gnomes and Dwarves chase technology and siege engines, the elves and Draenei favor culture and faith, Goblins build markets and banks, Humans stay balanced. Alliance races are more diplomatic and loyal, so they tend to befriend one another more often; Horde races are quicker to go to war.

**16 city-states:** Dalaran, Theramore, Stromgarde, Gadgetzan, Booty Bay, Ratchet, Everlook, Steamwheedle Port, Light's Hope Chapel, Cenarion Hold, Argent Vanguard, Feathermoon Stronghold, the Earthen Ring, the Knights of the Ebon Blade, the Explorers' League and the Thorium Brotherhood.

**10 religions:** Holy Light, Elune, Earth Mother, Shamanism, Loa, Cenarion Circle, August Celestials, Titans, Forgotten Shadow, Light of the Sunwell.

**Warcraft names throughout**, drawn from the Warcraft II/III strategy games and World of Warcraft: techs (Iron, Steel and Mithril Forged Swords, Animal War Training, Long Rifles, Storm Hammers, Liquid Fire, Eye of Kilrogg…), units (Peasant, Militia, Ballista, Gryphon Rider, Mortar Team, Siege Engine, Demolisher, Flying Machine, Giant Turtle, Juggernaught…), buildings (Town Hall, Keep, Blacksmith, Arcane Sanctum, Mage Tower, Shipyard, Guard Tower, Beastiary, Auction House, Coin Forge, Hall of Relics…), policies and unit types without modern terms, 40 world wonders (Karazhan, Ulduar, Icecrown Citadel, Zul'Gurub…), resources (Thorium, Azerite, Mageweave, Truesilver…), and Great People named after each race's own heroes.

## Installing

**In Unciv (any device):** open *Mods*, search for **UncivWarcraft** and press *Download*. Or use *Download mod from URL* and paste this repository's GitHub address.

**Desktop, manually:** download this repository as a ZIP, unzip it, rename the folder to `UncivWarcraft`, and put it in Unciv's `mods` folder.

Then start a new game, choose **UncivWarcraft** as the base ruleset, and pick the *Custom* map type: the Azeroth map is preselected.

## Building from source

The mod is generated from Unciv's own Gods & Kings ruleset by the scripts in [`tools/`](tools/TOOLS.md), so it can be rebuilt for new Unciv versions. Edit the data files there (races, names, map layout) rather than the generated JSON.
